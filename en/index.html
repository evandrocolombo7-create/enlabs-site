/ 02 — Studio About

enlabs CGI for Architecture and Real Estate Developments is an architectural visualization studio working at the frontier between design and image. Founded by Evandro Colombo, an architect and 3D artist with a degree in Architecture and Urban Planning from UNIMAR, the studio grew out of a path that went through executive design, construction sites, and BIM before turning entirely to visualization. That background defines how enlabs works: every project starts with a reading of the architecture itself — its floor plans, proportions, and intentions — not just the geometry to be rendered. The studio partners with architects, developers, and industry clients, translating projects into images and animations built on form, light, atmosphere, and concept. More than a technical deliverable, each visualization is treated as a piece with presence, one that lets people explore, feel, and communicate a project before it exists. At enlabs, visualization is understood as part of the design process itself — a way of helping people perceive architecture through the intention behind it.
