About

I'm Evandro Colombo, an architect and 3D artist working at the threshold between design and visualization. I graduated in Architecture and Urban Planning from UNIMAR, in the city of Marília, and my path went through executive design, construction sites, and BIM before focusing entirely on architectural visualization. I founded StudioEn Arquitetura alongside my wife, also an architect, and soon after, EnLabs, my own ArchViz studio, where I worked with architects, developers, and industry clients. Those two professional ventures — or companies, if you prefer to call them that — no longer exist, since I decided to work as an independent 3D artist, supporting other studios, and to prioritize deepening my technical knowledge and research in 3D and CGI over entrepreneurship. I like to think of architectural design as something beyond function. A piece that brings a visual impact into the world, one that can be explored early through 3D. That exploration gives me the ability to impact other people and prompt them to perceive architecture through the intention behind its form, light, atmosphere, and concept. On this site, I'm sharing a bit of that journey publicly.
